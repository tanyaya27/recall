# UX board, 2026-09-19: People, roles, and Peter's first minute

Maya (copy) and Devin (reduce). Premise as settled in `BOARD_2026-09-19_multi-user_reset.md` §10, not re-argued. On-screen copy is in *italics*. Confirms (title · body · keep · action) and toasts use the ThingCard pattern.

## Phase 1 — People, the invite, Peter's first minute

**Hamburger.** One new row, second: *Text size & colours · People · Places · Deleted items · Research log*. Devin wanted it last so her rows stay put; Maya: "who can see my things" belongs near the top. Split 1.

**People screen** (Header *People*, chevron Back). Empty state, one card: *Nobody else can see your things yet.*, the primary button *Invite someone…*, and one quiet line: *Anyone you invite will see the photos of your things and where they are — except things you mark Only me.* That is the consent line from the reset note §2; it appears again in the invite sheet and nowhere else.

With people, a list. Each row: name (from their sign-in), a quiet second line with role and origin — *Can help · invited Sep 12*, *Can look · joined by link Sep 14* — and a chevron. No avatars, no icons. Tapping a row opens a sheet titled with the name: a segmented control *Can look / Can help* (as Text size does it), *Remove Peter* in amber, *Cancel*. Role changes on release, toast *Peter can now help*. Remove confirms: *Remove Peter?* / *He will no longer see your things. Nothing is deleted.* / *Keep* / *Remove*; toast *Peter removed*. Pending invitations are rows too: *Invitation sent · Sep 12 · not opened yet*, tap → *Send again* / *Cancel invitation*. Below the list, *Invite someone…*, then the Phase 3 switch.

**The role words.** Drive's *Viewer / Editor* are known to Drive users; Margaret is not one, and "editor" is a newspaper job. The reset note's *Can see / Can help* is close, but *see* collides with the app's own verb (*Find item* answers "where do I see it"). We recommend **Can look / Can help**: two words, same length, verbs she uses about people. Each option carries one explaining line, and that line is the real definition:

- *Can look — sees your things and where they are. Cannot change anything.*
- *Can help — can also add photos, move things and fix names.*

*Owner* never appears on her screen; it is her ReCall. The data still says viewer/editor/owner; the words are skin.

**Invite sheet.** Title *Invite someone*. The two role options with their lines (preselection: split 2), the consent line, and one primary button *Send a link…* which opens the system share sheet (Messages, WhatsApp); nothing is typed in the app. No name field: the name comes from Peter's sign-in, so it cannot be mis-spelled.

**The text message** (what Peter receives):

> Margaret has invited you to help with her ReCall — the photos of where her things are. Open this link on your phone: recall.app/j/7fK2q
> It works for 7 days.

"help" or "look at" follows the role. No app-store paragraph; the link handles installed / not installed.

**Peter's first minute**, three taps:

1. Taps the link. Page: *Margaret's ReCall* large, one line *Margaret has shared the photos of where her things are. Sign in so she knows it's you.*, buttons *Continue with Apple · Continue with Google*. (Split 3.)
2. Taps a provider; the OS does the rest.
3. Lands on the grid, title line *Margaret's ReCall* where his own would show the day line. One-time toast: *You can now help Margaret* (or *look at Margaret's things*).

Expired or used link: *This invitation has expired. Ask Margaret to send a new one.* No retry button.

**Peter with no ReCall of his own.** He never sees an empty "mine" grid. His app opens on Margaret's; the hamburger title reads *Margaret's ReCall* with one extra row at the bottom, *Start my own ReCall*. A second grid exists only once he has logged a thing of his own.

**Switching.** With more than one grid, the title line becomes a tap target: *Margaret's ReCall ▾* → sheet *My ReCall · Margaret's ReCall*. The app reopens on whichever was last open. No tabs, no swipe.

**Grid tag.** A thing someone else owns shows *Robert's* under the name in the tile label, quiet colour, words not colour. It appears in Margaret's grid only for one-off shares into hers; in Peter's view of *Margaret's ReCall* nothing is tagged.

## Phase 2 — the card per role

**Viewer (Can look).** Chevron · name · no lock (private things are not there). Place band, roll, no trash on photos. Switches: *Show times on photos · Show earlier places* only. No bar. The grid footer is *Find item* alone, full width. A card with nothing to do is a photo and a place, and that is right for Priya at a distance.

**Editor (Can help).** The owner's card minus the *Shared with* row (a static line *Shared by Margaret* instead) and minus *Remove*, so the bar is *Add photo · Edit*. The photo trash and *Remove old photos…* stay (tidy).

**Owner.** *Keep this private* becomes a row **Shared with · Peter, Robert** (or *Only me*) with a chevron, first in the switch list. The lock in the title band stays and means *Only me*. The sheet, titled with the thing's name:

- Whole-ReCall people, greyed, role shown, second line *via your ReCall*; a hint under the group: *Change these in People.*
- One-off people, each with a tappable role (*Can look / Can help*) and swipe-left *Remove*.
- *Add a person…* → the invite sheet, pre-scoped to this thing (message: *…invited you to help with one of her things: the good scissors*).
- *Only me*, a switch at the bottom: on = private. Confirm when people are listed: *Keep the scissors to yourself?* / *Peter and Robert will no longer see it. Photos they have already seen stay seen.* / *Keep sharing* / *Only me*. Toast *Only you can see this*.
- *Transfer to…*, last row, quiet; picks from her People. Confirm: *Give the scissors to Robert?* / *It moves to Robert's ReCall with all its photos. You will still be able to help with it.* / *Keep it* / *Give it*. Toast *The scissors are now Robert's*. (Split 4.)

**The *added by* switch.** In People, not on the card: *Show who added each photo*, off by default. When on, each photo's fixed-size time label gains a name: *Today 5:52 PM · Robert*. Nothing else changes. One switch in People is one decision; a switch per card was one more thing on every card.

## Phase 3 — the status line

In People, under the list: switch *Let my people see when I last used the app*, off. Explaining line: *They will see one line: when you last opened ReCall and how many things you logged today.* After a *Can look* invite is sent, one quiet suggestion: *Peter can look but not change. Want him to see when you last used the app?* with *Yes* / *Not now* — the "in her words" of reset split 4.

Peter sees, on the grid under *Margaret's ReCall*: *Opened today 4:02 pm · 3 things logged*; nothing logged: *Opened today 4:02 pm*; not today: *Last opened Tuesday*. No colour, no alarm state; a line, not a monitor. When she turns it off, the line goes.

## The upgrade moment

Asked once, at the first *Send a link…*, before the share sheet: title *Sign in to share*, body *So your things stay yours on any phone, sign in once. Everything you have logged stays.*, buttons *Continue with Apple · Continue with Google · Not now*. On *Not now*: the invite sheet closes, nothing is sent, toast *Invite when you're ready — it's under People*. No nag, no badge; the next tap on *Invite someone…* asks again. This is the one screen that can lose her, so: one sentence, no password. Same prompt when a second device is added, later.

## Removal and revoke

Owner removes Peter (above). Peter's next open: Margaret's grid is gone; with no ReCall of his own he sees one card, *Margaret's ReCall is no longer shared with you.* with *Start my own ReCall*. No reason, no "ask her" button; she chose. Peter can also leave: hamburger row *Leave Margaret's ReCall* → *Leave Margaret's ReCall?* / *You can be invited again.* / *Stay* / *Leave*. Her People row becomes *Peter left · Sep 20*, swipe to clear. An unopened link is revoked by *Cancel invitation* on its row; the link then shows the expired copy.

## The words

**ReCall for "my collection".** It works because she has one and never names it; *Margaret's ReCall* reads as "Margaret's app". It strains only in *Start my own ReCall* and *Leave Margaret's ReCall*, where the product name doubles as a container. Alternatives: *things* (*Margaret's things*) — plain, but "share my things" sounds like lending; *collection* — museum. Recommendation: keep **ReCall** as the container, use *things* in every explaining line.

**People.** Alternatives: *Sharing* (Drive), *Family* (Priya is not family), *Who can see* (a sentence, and helpers do more than see). Recommendation: **People**; the explaining lines do the defining.

**Roles.** *Can look / Can help*, as argued.

## Splits

1. **People's position in the hamburger.** Maya: second. Devin: last. Recommendation: second; it is the row the spouse looks for at setup, and Places, Deleted, Research keep their order below it.
2. **Default role in the invite sheet.** Maya: *Can help* preselected for the first invite (usually the spouse who set the phone up). Devin: no preselection; a tapped role is a role she knows she gave. Recommendation: no preselection; *Send a link…* is disabled until one is tapped.
3. **Explaining line on Peter's sign-in page.** Devin: none. Maya: one; "so she knows it's you" pre-empts "why sign in for photos". Recommendation: one.
4. **Transfer row in the card sheet.** Devin: absent until built. Maya: designed now so the sheet has a bottom. Recommendation: in the mock, greyed; absent from the first build.
5. **Editor's bar.** Devin: *Add photo* only, since a helper mostly re-photographs. Maya: fixing the name and moving the thing are half of "help". Recommendation: *Add photo · Edit*.
6. **Status line on the card too.** Devin: grid only. Maya: also on the card. Recommendation: grid only.

## Summary

People is one new hamburger row with a list, an invite sheet with two roles in plain words, *Can look / Can help*, and one consent sentence said twice.
Peter's first minute is link → Apple/Google → *Margaret's ReCall*, three taps, and he never sees an empty grid of his own.
The card per role removes rather than adds: viewers get photo and place; editors lose *Remove* and sharing; the owner's *Keep this private* becomes *Shared with · names* with *Only me* and, later, *Transfer to…*.
Sign-in is asked once, at the first share, in one sentence, with *Not now* that costs nothing.
Keep the words *ReCall* and *People*; say *things* when explaining.

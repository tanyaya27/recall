# Shutter mark: which choice the shutter remembers (design round, 2026-09-30)

Sheets (drawn over the real camera screens in the rig, not built):
- `DESIGN_2026-09-30_shutter-overview.jpg`: the four options side by side (modal rows, (b), (c), 3× close-ups)
- `DESIGN_2026-09-30_shutter-R1.jpg`, `-R2.jpg`, `-A.jpg`, `-B.jpg`: each sheet shows the modal, (a), (b), (d), the Choose place sheet, (c), (b) on a bright scene, 3× close-ups, and the modal rows at 1.5×

**Recommendation: A, "glyph in the disc".** The white disc gets one dark glyph: a photo stack for "more photos of this place" and a pin for "the new place". Nothing sits above the ring. The same glyph, on a 44 pt mini shutter, leads the matching row in "This photo is…" and in Choose place. A takes Ravi's pin-in-the-disc from option 1 as it is. It takes Ravi's "mark inside the white disc" from option 2 but swaps the "+" for a photo stack. It drops both of Ravi's tabs.

---

## 1. Why the rejected chip fails (OPTIONS_2026-09-30_shutter-chip.jpg)

1. **It is a notification badge.** A small dark capsule sits on the top edge of a round button, with a coloured stroke. That is exactly how iOS and Android draw "3 unread" badges. The yellow "+1" blob in the fly-in frame is literally a badge.
2. **It sits on the seam.** The shutter's ring top is about 8 pt below the bottom of the camera image, and the prompt card ends about 10 pt above that. So the chip straddles three surfaces: the photo, the card edge and the black bar. The top half changes with whatever the camera sees, and the bottom half is black. The chip has no ground of its own, so it looks pasted on.
3. **The contents are illegible.** A 22 pt thumbnail of a cardboard box at phone distance is a brown smudge. The "+" is a 15 px heavy glyph crammed against it. "NEW" is small, letter-spaced caps in mint inside an amber stroke, which is three colour families in 20 pt and reads like a sticker.
4. **It adds a second amber stroke.** The ring is already amber. An amber-outlined pill on top of it makes two strokes of the same colour at different weights, touching. It looks like a mistake, not a hierarchy.
5. **It is not in the modal.** She makes the decision in "This photo is…", but the mark first appears later on the shutter. Nothing ties the two together, which is Ravi's point.
6. **The fly-in reads as a diagram.** A dashed line and two ghost thumbnails look like a storyboard arrow, not motion. They also land on top of the "White cardboard box" chip and the "Place:" line, which makes both unreadable.

## 2. The four options

All four are rendered on the real screens: Ravi's seeded house, "3D model of plant sensor", Move it, level 1 (amber). Every option uses the same (d) mechanism so that only the shutter mark is compared.

### R1: Ravi option 1 (place's photo in the disc + "+" tab; pin in the disc + "New" tab)
- **What:** in (b), the disc becomes the White cardboard box's photo inside a 2.5 pt white rim, with a frosted "+" tab riding the top of the ring. In (c), a white disc holds a dark pin, with a "New" tab. In the modal, the rows lead with a mini shutter carrying the same thing.
- **Strong:** the photo is the most specific answer to "which place?". **The pin in the disc for (c) is the best single idea in this round**, and it is carried into A unchanged.
- **Weak:**
  - A photo inside a round button is the camera's "last photo / open gallery" control on every phone. It reads as "look at photos", not "take one".
  - A dark photo turns the white disc into mud: in the (b) close-up the shutter stops reading as a shutter.
  - The "+" tab is a second "+" on the screen with a different meaning. The strip's "+", 60 pt above, means "add a level".
  - Both tabs sit on the seam and read as badges (the same failure as section 1).
  - In the modal, the place photo shrinks from 44 pt to a 32 pt circle and becomes hard to recognise. That is the one moment she needs to compare "is this the same box?".

### R2: Ravi option 2 ("+" / "New" in the disc; place photo / pin as the tab)
- **What:** in (b), a white disc with a dark "+" and a 34×28 pt place-photo tab on the ring. In (c), a white disc with the word "New" and a pin tab.
- **Strong:** the white disc survives, so the shutter still says "shoot". A mark inside the disc is the right place: it is always on the black bar, so it works over bright and dark scenes alike (compare the bright close-ups).
- **Weak:**
  - "+" again collides with add-a-level.
  - "New" is text inside a shutter. It reads as a button label, and "new what?" (a new photo? a new item?) is ambiguous at 15 pt.
  - The photo tab is an avatar-on-a-button, the notification pattern, and it is the least calm of the four.
  - In the modal, the row needs a 20 pt tab above a 44 pt token, which is fussy at row size.

### A: Glyph in the disc (mine, recommended)
- **What:**
  - (b): a photo-stack glyph (a photo with one more peeking behind, up and to the right) in the white disc.
  - (c): the app's own pin in the white disc.
  - (a): the plain disc.
  - No tab, no caption.
  - The modal's "Another photo of the White cardboard box" row leads with a 44 pt mini shutter carrying the stack and keeps the place's 44 pt photo at the trailing edge. "A different place" leads with the mini shutter carrying the pin. "Photograph a new place" in Choose place swaps its camera icon for the pin mini shutter.
- **Why it works:**
  - It is one element that never leaves the disc. The disc stays white and round, so it is still "take a photo" (the same move as the phone camera's shutter changing when you switch it to video).
  - The glyph is meaning by shape. The ring colour stays the tier's, so nothing rests on colour alone.
  - The stack glyph rhymes with the (d) feedback. After each shot, the tier square gains a stack edge peeking up and to the right, which is the same geometry as the glyph. The whole (b) story is "you're adding to a pile of photos", told the same way in the modal, on the shutter and on the square.
  - The prompt line keeps the words.
- **Risks:**
  - A glyph must be learned. The modal teaches it at the moment she chooses, and the prompt line spells it out ("Shutter: more photos of…").
  - The photo stack could be read as "gallery". It only appears after she picked "Another photo of…", and it sits inside the ring, where no gallery button lives. **Test with Tanya.**
  - The pin could be read as "location/GPS". In this app the pin already means "a place" (the ask line, the empty-tier icon).

### B: Caption above the shutter (mine)
- **What:** the shutter is untouched. A frosted 28 pt caption in words docks 9 pt above the ring: "[place thumb] More photos" or "[pin] New place". The prompt card lifts 48 pt to make room. In the modal, the same caption sits as a third line under each choice. In Choose place, it sits at the trailing edge of the mint button.
- **Strong:** words need no learning, and the frosted material stays legible on the bright closet scene. The shutter stays completely pure.
- **Weak:**
  - It says the same thing as the prompt line 60 pt higher, so there are two labels for one fact.
  - It floats over the camera image and costs 48 pt of viewfinder whenever a choice is remembered.
  - It makes the modal rows taller and the Choose place button crowded.
  - Honestly, it is the rejected chip done well rather than a new idea.

## 3. Recommendation: A

A is the only option where the mark is **inside the shutter's own ground** (the black bar), so it is immune to what the camera sees and never touches the seam. It adds **no new object** to a busy screen, and the shutter still reads as a shutter. The mark is **the same object in the modal**: a small shutter with the same glyph, so the association Ravi asked for is literal ("this row makes your shutter look like this").

Ravi's instincts were right on two counts: a mark belongs *in the disc* (option 2), and the *pin* is the right sign for "new place" (option 1). Two parts of his options are what make them look like badges and should go: the tabs above the ring, and the "+", which this screen already uses for "add a level".

## 4. Specs for A

All sizes are in pt (CSS px). The ink is `#1C1A17` on `#FFFFFF`, a contrast of 17.4:1.

### Shutter (geometry unchanged)
| part | value |
|---|---|
| outer | 80 pt; ring 5 pt in the selected tier colour (level 0 = white); 6 pt black gap; disc 58 pt `#FFF`; hit area 100 pt circle (as built) |
| (a) | plain disc, no glyph |
| (b) photo stack | 30 pt box on a 24-unit grid; stroke 2.1 units (≈2.6 pt), round caps and joins, `currentColor` = ink. Front photo `rect x3.5 y7.5 w13 h13 rx2.5`; back edge `M7.5 3.5h11a2 2 0 0 1 2 2v11`; sun `circle 12.2,11.6 r1.25` filled; hill `M3.8 18.6l4.2-4.1 3 2.8`. Optical nudge +0.5 pt x, −0.5 pt y (the mass sits bottom-left). |
| (c) pin | the app's PinIcon (`M12 21s-6-5.5-6-11a6 6 0 0 1 12 0c0 5.5-6 11-6 11z` + `circle 12,10 r2.5`), 27 pt box, stroke 2.2 units (≈2.5 pt); optical nudge +1 pt y (the pin's mass sits high) |
| glyph share | 46–52% of the disc diameter; never tier-coloured, never filled with a photo |
| pressed | disc scale 0.94 (existing), glyph scales with it |

### Mini shutter token (modal rows and Choose place)
- 44 pt circle with a `#000` fill and a 3 pt inset ring in the tier colour. The disc is 32 pt `#FFF` and the glyph 19 pt (stack) or 18 pt (pin) at 2.3 units, with the same nudges scaled. Proportions match the real shutter (disc/outer = 0.727). A 0.5 pt `rgba(255,255,255,.12)` hairline keeps the black edge visible on the dark sheet.
- **"This photo is…":**
  - `pf-same` is laid out as: token (leading) · gap 12 · text · place photo 44 pt, radius 10 (trailing). The title may wrap to 2 lines. The row keeps its 2 pt tier-colour border.
  - `pf-other`: the token (pin) replaces the list icon.
  - Row min-height stays ≥ 56 pt, and the whole row remains the button.
- **Choose place:** the token (pin) replaces the camera icon in "Photograph a new place". Set padding-left to 8 pt and the gap to 12 pt. The token's black disc gives it contrast on the mint.
- The token is decorative: `aria-hidden="true"`.

### The prompt line (words; as built, plus (c))
- (b): "Shutter: more photos of the White cardboard box. Tap its square to change that."
- (c): "Shutter: the new place. Photograph it."
- After each shot in (b), for 2.5 s: "Added — 3 photos of the White cardboard box. Tap its square to change that." Then it returns to the (b) line.

### (d) Feedback after each shot in (b)
- **Fly-in:**
  - A 58 pt thumbnail of the shot, with a 1.5 pt white keyline and shadow `0 6 18 rgba(0,0,0,.45)`, leaves the disc centre and lands on the tier square's centre.
  - It follows a gentle arc (control point 26 pt to the right of the chord's middle).
  - Timing is 320 ms, `cubic-bezier(0.3, 0.7, 0.2, 1)`. Over the flight the corner radius goes from 29 to 12 and the size goes from 58 pt to the square's size.
  - There are no trails, dashes or ghosts.
- **Arrival:**
  - The square's photo crossfades to the new shot (120 ms).
  - The square does one 1.00 → 1.05 → 1.00 pulse over 220 ms, `cubic-bezier(.34, 1.56, .64, 1)`.
  - A **stack edge** fades in (0 → 0.6 over 160 ms): a copy of the square's outline offset 5 pt up and to the right, with a 2 pt stroke in the tier colour at 60% on `#2D2A27`, radius 14, clipped so only the peeking L shows.
  - The stack edge then stays on any square whose place has 2 or more photos, so it is a quiet, permanent "this is a pile" signal and not a counter.
- **No numbers on the square.** The count lives in words on the prompt line and in VoiceOver.
- **Haptic:** a light impact on iOS (native wrapper) and `navigator.vibrate(8)` on Android.

### Entering and leaving (b)/(c)
- **Enter**, after the sheet closes (250 ms): the glyph goes opacity 0 → 1 and scale 0.6 → 1.0 over 220 ms, `cubic-bezier(0.2, 0.8, 0.2, 1)`, after an 80 ms delay. The ring does not change.
- **Leave** (she taps a tier or the level changes): opacity 1 → 0 and scale 1 → 0.8 over 140 ms, ease-in.
- **Reduced motion** (`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`):
  - No fly-in, no pulse and no scale.
  - The glyph only fades (150 ms).
  - The square crossfades to the new shot (150 ms) and the stack edge appears without motion.
  - The words on the prompt line carry the feedback.

### VoiceOver
- Shutter label, by state:
  - (a): "Take a photo".
  - (b): "Take a photo. Adds to the White cardboard box."
  - (c): "Take a photo of the new place."
- Shutter hint in (b)/(c): "To change this, tap the level's square."
- After each (b) shot, announce politely (`aria-live="polite"`): "Photo added to the White cardboard box. 3 photos."
- Modal rows: labels as built. Add to `pf-same` the description "Later photos on this level go to the White cardboard box too." Add to `pf-other` the description "The next photo becomes the new place."

### Checks the renders pass
- The mark never leaves the black bar, so it reads the same on the dark storage scene and the bright closet scene. See the "(b) bright 3×" close-up on the A sheet.
- Nothing sits on the ring or the seam. There is no number and no dot, so there is no badge reading.
- Meaning rests on glyph shape plus words, never on colour alone.
- Touch targets are unchanged: shutter 100 pt, rows ≥ 56 pt.

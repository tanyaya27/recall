# Is "ReCall" available as the app's name? First findings (2026-09-24)

A spot check done in one session, **not a trademark clearance** (only an attorney's search is).
Sources are linked so the next session can re-check rather than trust this.

## Summary

**"ReCall" on its own is very unlikely to be available as an App Store name, and it's a crowded,
weak name to build a brand on.** The words under the icon on the home screen can still say *ReCall*
whatever the store name is.

## The App Store

- Apple requires the **store listing name to be unique across the whole App Store**. The name under
  the icon (`CFBundleDisplayName`) does not have to be unique
  ([PTKD](https://ptkd.com/journal/can-two-apps-have-the-same-name-app-store)). The definitive test is
  reserving the name in App Store Connect after enrolling.
- Apps already listed (US store, 09-24):
  - [Recall](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/recall/id1224074924), a memory puzzle game (Telphasa)
  - [Recall-](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/recall/id6466136108), "the non-calendar, calendar app" (Brian Masse)
  - [Recall: Daily notes](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/recall-daily-notes/id6736586960)
  - [Recall Notes](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/recall-notes/id6745151647)
  - [ReCall – Call Recorder](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/recall-call-recorder/id1637632841), with our exact spelling
  - [RECALL](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/recall/id1231598152)
  - [Recall – Efficient Flashcards](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/recall-efficient-flashcards/id1449434101)
- Not verified: whether Apple treats *ReCall* and *Recall* as the same name (it probably does).

## Products in the same space

- [Recall: Summarize & save](https://apps.apple.com/app/recall-summarize-save/id6445893722)
  ([recall.it](https://www.recall.it/faq)): an AI app for saving and recalling content, very close
  to "an app for your memory".
- [Windows Recall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Windows_Recall): Microsoft's AI feature that
  records the screen, widely criticised on privacy. An app that photographs the inside of people's
  homes should not share its name.
- [Recall.ai](https://www.recall.ai/): a funded company making meeting-recording software.

## Why it matters more now

Tanya's 09-24 ruling makes ReCall a **general memory app**. For that product, "recall" comes close
to *describing* it, and descriptive names are the hardest to protect and the easiest to lose in
search. A distinctive name is worth deciding before the App Store listing, the identity brief
(`03_Design/ReCall_Identity_Design_Prompt.md`) and any domain.
